BantayDavao

Every Dabawenyo becomes a bantay, a guard who protects the family from the season's threats. Real-life safety actions become daily quests with XP, levels and streaks.

  • Daily safety questsWater, shade, checking on lolo and lola, clearing drains. Earn XP, levels and streaks.
  • Live heat index and rain warningsPAGASA's Davao City heat index, a live hourly forecast and PAGASA rainfall warnings.
  • Pamilya: family locationYour family on one map. During floods and heat, tap "I'm safe" or "I need help".
  • Offline map of help nearbyEvacuation centers, hospitals, water stations and flood-prone barangays, even without signal.
  • First aid and hotlinesDOH steps, danger signs that mean "Call 911", and one-tap city hotlines.
  • Share warnings and resultsSend warnings, first-aid steps and your progress to family chats as a picture.
  • Phone remindersQuests, water and a morning heat or rain heads-up, even when the app is closed.
  • English, Tagalog and BisayaFree, no ads, and no account needed except for Pamilya.
