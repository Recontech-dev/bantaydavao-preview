Privacy Policy

BantayDavao by Recontech · Last updated 24 October 2026

BantayDavao is a free safety app for Davao City made by Recontech, Davao City, Philippines. This page explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. We wrote it in plain words on purpose.

Our promise

  • We never sell your information, and we never rent, trade or give it to advertisers, data brokers or anyone else.
  • BantayDavao is completely free. No payments, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no paid features. You will never be asked for money or card details.
  • No ads, no tracking. The app has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking tools, and does not build a profile about you.
  • No account needed. The whole app works without signing in. Linking Google is optional and only keeps a backup of your progress.
  • Most of your data never leaves your phone. Your XP, quests, streaks, name and photo stay on your device unless you share them or link Google to back them up.
  • Family location is only for your family. If you use Pamilya, only the people in your family can see your location, and only its latest point is kept.
  • You stay in control. You can pause sharing, leave your family, or delete your account and its data at any time, inside the app.

Sa madaling salita

Libre ang BantayDavao: walang bayad, walang ads, at hindi namin ibinebenta o ipinamimigay ang impormasyon mo kahit kanino. Halos lahat ng data mo ay nasa telepono mo lang. Kapag ginamit mo ang Pamilya, ang mga kapamilya mo lang na inimbita mo ang makakakita ng lokasyon mo, at puwede mo itong i-pause o burahin anumang oras.

1. Information that stays on your phone

These are saved in the app's storage on your device. We cannot see them, unless you link Google to keep a backup (section 3).

  • Your display name, district and optional profile photo
  • Quests, XP, levels, streaks, water glasses and history
  • Your settings: language, theme and which reminders you want
  • In-app notifications and the reminders scheduled on your phone

They are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. If you use Save backup, the app makes a file and you choose where it goes (for example your Files or Google Drive). That file is yours; we never receive it.

2. Location

Mapa ("Locate me")

When you tap Locate me, the app asks your phone for your position to show nearby help. It is used on your phone only and is not sent to us.

Pamilya (family location), only if you join a family

  • While BantayDavao is open, your phone sends your current position to our family service every few minutes, so your family can see you on their map.
  • Only your latest position is stored. Each new one replaces the old one, so there is no location history.
  • The app does not use your location in the background. When the app is closed, nothing is sent.
  • You can turn off Share my location at any time. Your family then sees "Paused".

3. Accounts: guest or Google, both optional

You can use all of BantayDavao without an account.

  • Guest account. The first time you create or join a family in Pamilya, the app makes a guest account for you automatically. It has no name, email or password; it is only a random ID kept on your phone. If you delete the app or lose your phone, a guest account cannot be recovered.
  • Linking Google (optional). In Settings → Account you can link your Google account. Then the app keeps a backup copy of your progress (XP, quests, streaks, water, settings, display name and optional photo), and signing in with Google on a new phone brings it back together with your family. The backup is updated when your progress changes. It never includes your location.

4. Services the app connects to

To work, BantayDavao talks to a few outside services. None of them receive your name, your location or your app data from us, unless noted.

  • Supabase (database and sign-in, servers in Singapore): stores the account data in section 3, only if you use Pamilya or link Google.
  • Google: handles sign-in when you link Google. Google's own privacy policy applies to your Google account.
  • Open-Meteo (weather forecast): the app asks for the forecast for fixed Davao City coordinates, never your own location.
  • PAGASA: the app downloads PAGASA's public heat index bulletin.
  • Google Maps: only if you tap Directions. Google Maps opens with that place as the destination.

Like any website, these services can see your phone's internet (IP) address when the app connects to them. We do not combine it with anything or use it to identify you.

The map of Davao City is built into the app, so viewing it sends nothing anywhere.

5. Sharing is always your choice

When you tap a Share button (warnings, first-aid results, your daily progress or a family invite), your phone's share menu opens and you pick the app and the people. Nothing is shared unless you do this.

6. Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone itself. There is no notification server, and you can turn each kind off in Settings or in your phone's settings.

7. How we protect your information

  • All connections use encryption (HTTPS).
  • Family data is protected by database rules so that only your family can read it.
  • We keep as little as possible: no location history, no message logs, no analytics.
  • Only the people at Recontech who run the service can manage the database, and only to keep it working or to act on your request.

We will never knowingly share your information. If a security problem ever affects your data, we will tell affected users and the National Privacy Commission as the law requires.

8. How long we keep it, and deleting it

  • Leave family (in Pamilya) removes your location, check-in and membership from that family.
  • Delete account (Settings → Account) permanently deletes your account, its backup and all your Pamilya data. Your XP and progress stay on your phone.
  • Sign out keeps your backup and family for when you sign in with Google again.
  • If a family's owner leaves or deletes their account, the member who joined earliest becomes the new owner. When the last member leaves, the family itself is deleted.
  • You can also ask us to delete your data by email (below). We will do it within 7 days.

9. Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you have the right to be informed, to access your information, to correct it, to object, to have it deleted, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. Most of these you can do inside the app. For anything else, email us.

10. Children

BantayDavao is made for families, and the quests are safe for all ages. Children should use Pamilya with a parent or guardian, who can manage the family and remove members at any time. Linking Google follows Google's own age rules.

11. Not a government app

BantayDavao is made by Recontech. It is not an official app of the City Government of Davao, PAGASA or the Department of Health. In an emergency, call 911.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app before they apply. We will never start selling data or charging money.
