1. What BantayDavao is

BantayDavao helps families in Davao City get ready for heat, rain, floods and polluted air with daily quests, warnings, a map of nearby help, first aid tips and family check-ins. It is made by Recontech. It is not an official app of the City Government of Davao, PAGASA, DENR or the Department of Health.

2. Emergencies

The app does not call for help on its own and does not watch over you. If someone is in danger, call 911 or go to the nearest hospital. Use the hotlines in Tabang only as a convenience; numbers can change.

3. Warnings, forecasts and air quality are a guide

  • Heat index levels follow PAGASA. Rainfall warnings follow PAGASA when they are available and may be set by hand while no data feed exists.
  • The forecast (Open-Meteo) and air quality (a CAMS model estimate rated with DENR levels) are estimates. They can differ from what you see outside.
  • Saved information used offline can be old. The app shows when it was last updated.
  • Always follow official announcements and the instructions of your barangay and the CDRRMO, especially about evacuation.

4. Health information is not medical advice

First aid steps follow DOH guidance in simple words. The photo check and the list of signs only suggest what to watch for; they cannot tell what someone has. Danger signs mean: call 911 or go to a hospital now. Ask a doctor or health worker before relying on any tip.

5. Maps, places and directions

Map data comes from OpenStreetMap and can be incomplete or out of date. Places marked "Sample" are placeholders until the City confirms them. Before you go somewhere, especially during a flood, check that the road is safe and the place is open. Directions open Google Maps, which has its own terms.

6. Pamilya and accounts

  • Only invite people you know, and only share your own location. Never add someone without their OK.
  • Check-ins ("I'm safe" or "I need help") are messages to your family, not to rescuers. If you need rescue, call 911.
  • You are responsible for keeping your family code private. You can remove members, leave a family or delete your account at any time in the app.
  • Children should use Pamilya with a parent or guardian.

7. Fair use

Please don't: send false or alarming messages, pretend to be the City, PAGASA or a rescuer, harass anyone, try to get into other people's families or data, overload or break the service, or copy the app to trick people. We may remove access for anyone who does.

8. XP, levels and badges

XP, levels, streaks and badges reward safe habits. They are free, cannot be bought or sold, and have no money value. They may be adjusted if a bug gives too much or too little.

9. Free app, changes and availability

BantayDavao is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases. We may improve, change or stop features, and the online services it uses may sometimes be down. Core safety information stays available offline once saved.
